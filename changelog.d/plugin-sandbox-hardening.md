### Plugins

- **Contract change: plugin permissions** - `plugin.json` declares what a plugin reaches beyond HTTP to the internet: `"permissions": {"lan": true, "mqtt": true, "modbus": true}`. `lan` allows HTTP to local addresses (checked after DNS resolution), `mqtt` the plugin's MQTT methods, `modbus` the `Modbus` table. A plugin without the field gets none of them; an official plugin (from the official repository, or installed by hand in `@vclu`) keeps all three, with a warning in the log, until its manifest declares them.
- **Contract change: a stricter sandbox** - a plugin gets its own copies of `string`, `table` and `math`; `Logger`, `JSON` and `Plugin` cannot be modified, and the `HTTP`, `HttpClient` and `MQTT` globals are gone (use `plugin:httpRequest`, `plugin:mqttSubscribe`). Objects a plugin did not create, other plugins included, are read-only also through `pairs`: only `get`, `getX`, `isX` and `hasX` methods can be called on them, and another plugin shows only its sensors and controls. `Plugin.getPlugin` returns the plugin's own instance; its id cannot be changed. No official plugin is affected.
- **`plugin:mqttPublish` works** - it passed `retain` and `qos` in the wrong order and failed on every call.

### Web

- **Press and hold, and a gate tile** - `opts.hold = true` (800 ms) or a time in ms (300-5000) on `section:button` and `section:toggle`: the action runs only after the tile is held that long (mouse, touch, or Enter/Space), with a ring filling around the icon; an early release or sliding off cancels, a tap shows "Przytrzymaj". The page sends `held: true` with the action and the server refuses it without; this stops stray taps and naive scripts, not someone sending the flag on purpose (use `:auth('pin')` or `'session'`). `hold` and `confirm` together fail the build; `confirm` works as before. New `section:gate(id, label, {open, close?, stop?, state?, hold, icon, disabled, span})`: the gate's state (open, closed, opening, closing, moving) in colour, Open and Close held (800 ms by default), Stop a tap ([#159](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/159)).

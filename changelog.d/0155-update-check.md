### Web

- **The panel says when a new vCLU version is out** - a badge "vCLU X.Y.Z" in the top right corner of every page opens each skipped release in short (its highlights and what to do before updating, the full notes behind "More") and how to update; "Hide until next version" hides it in this browser until a newer one. vCLU asks the update service 1-10 min after start and then every 6 h ± 30 min, sending only a random instance id (`instance-id` in the data directory), the version, os and arch. On by default; off with "Check for new versions" in Settings (no restart), `"updateCheck": {"enabled": false}` in `.vclu.json` or `VCLU_UPDATE_CHECK=off`; when off nothing is sent. New endpoints: `GET /api/update`, `PUT /api/update/settings` ([#155](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/155)).

### HomeKit

- **A thermostat is a Thermostat in Apple Home** - a `RemoteThermostat` or `VirtualThermostat` exposed as `climate` shows in the Home app with its current and target temperature and off/heat (cool for a cooling `VirtualThermostat`), the same as in Home Assistant. A target set in the Home app goes to `setSetpoint`, clamped to the expose `min`/`max`; the Home app's slider takes `min`, `max` and `step` from the expose options (default 5-35 °C, step 0.5), kept within 5-38 °C. A target that changes alone (holiday mode, the schedule) shows up too. The mode is shown, not set: off or heat picked in the Home app is put back, as Home Assistant has no mode command either. A read-only thermostat (`readonly = true` or the HomeKit access level) is still a Thermostat, with the target and the mode read-only. A thermostat already exposed as `climate` appears as a new accessory after the update (keep it out with `homekit = false`); the other accessories keep their ids, rooms and automations. The exposed list gains `climate` ([#62](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/62)).

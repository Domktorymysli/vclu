### Dashboards

- **Editing a schedule like selecting text** - a tap on a schedule's bar shows that stretch's hours and value in large type above the week. While editing, the tapped stretch gets two handles that move its ends (the hour shows over the finger, in the widget's steps), `-`/`+` buttons and time fields to the minute, its value as buttons, and "+ stretch" to add one in its middle. While editing, dragging the selected stretch moves it whole; a click outside the bars or Esc clears the selection. Painting half-hour cells is gone. The widget keeps its height when a stretch is tapped; a bar too narrow for its value shows only its colour; the module's windows outside the week (holidays, dates) are listed as exceptions, e.g. "On holidays 08:00–22:00: komfort".

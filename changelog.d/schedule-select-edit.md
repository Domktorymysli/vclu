### Dashboards

- **Editing a schedule like selecting text** - a tap on a schedule's bar shows that stretch's hours and value in large type above the week. While editing, the tapped stretch gets two handles that move its ends (the hour shows over the finger, in the widget's steps), `-`/`+` buttons and time fields to the minute, its value as buttons, and "+ stretch" to add one in its middle. Painting half-hour cells is gone.

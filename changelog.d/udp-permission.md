### Plugins

- **UDP to devices on the local network** - a new permission `udp` in `plugin.json` (plugin API 2) gives `plugin:udpSocket()`: a UDP socket with `send`, `broadcast` and `onReceive`, for devices with a local UDP protocol such as air conditioners. It needs no `clu`: it reaches only local addresses, never this vclu, and not the CLU ports (1234, 69), which stay with `clu` and `tftp`. It sends to the ports the plugin lists and broadcasts to those of them it marks (`"udp": {"ports": [7000], "broadcast": [7000]}`, up to 8). Installing allows it like `lan`, with the ports shown in the confirmation; the plugins page can limit it to some hosts, and then the plugin does not broadcast.

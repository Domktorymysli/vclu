### Lua runtime

- **`VirtualPresence` gives one presence per room from many sensors** - `VirtualPresence:new("lazienka", { trigger = { "satel.zone.3" }, keep = { radar, tv }, door = "satel.zone.7", hold = 120 })` is on while a trigger (motion, a button) is active, or a keep source (radar, TV, a socket in use) after a start, or a closed door once someone is seen inside after it closed ("wasp in a box": a trigger going on, or one still on `doorGrace` s (10) after the closing; leaving and closing it behind you does not keep the room), and for `hold` s (300) after the last ends; a new signal in the hold extends it with no second `OnSwitchOn`. A keep source or a door closed on an empty room never starts it. `getReason()` says what holds it, `getRemaining()` and `getDuration()` give seconds, `trigger(sec)` is a signal from code, `setOverride(1/0/nil)` forces present or empty, `setHold()` changes the hold. It starts empty, also after a reload, and has the interface of `VirtualSensor` (`DIN`). The hand-made motion timer in the AI examples is replaced by it ([#154](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/154)).
- **`expose(obj, "occupancy")`** - a Home Assistant `binary_sensor` with `device_class: occupancy` and a HomeKit occupancy sensor; `motion` stays as it is ([#154](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/154)).

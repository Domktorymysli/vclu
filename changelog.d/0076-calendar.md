### Lua runtime

- **A house calendar** - `Calendar.new('dom'):holidays('pl'):event(...):schedule(...):build()` describes the house's time in a module: events for people (`every = 'workday'`, `'tu'` with `weeks = 2`, `'month'` with `day = -1` or `on = 'last workday'`, `'year'` (a date, or `month = 10, on = 'last su'`), one-off dates and ranges, a list of days for a schedule published as dates (`dates = {...}`, e.g. waste collection), `except`, `moves`, reminders, tags, `jitter`) and schedules of values (`{ every = 'workday', hours = '13:00-15:00', value = 'tania' }`, the last window that covers the moment wins). Handlers `cal:on('start' | 'end' | 'reminder' | 'change', id, fn)`, queries `value()`, `isActive()`, `next()`, `today()`, `upcoming()`, `between(from, to)` and `day(date)` (dates or timestamps), and changes that survive a restart (`add`, `remove`, `skip`, `pause`). With `:location(lat, lon)` times can follow the sun (`time = 'sunset-30m'`, `'dusk'` and `'dawn'` for civil twilight, `hours = { 'sunset', 'sunrise' }`, `cal:sun(date)`), and a window can be the hours the sun stands on a facade (`sun = { azimuth = { 135, 225 }, elevation = 15 }`, `cal:sunPosition()`) for shading, computed in UTC so they are right in summer time too (the sun-position plugin's fixed `timezoneOffset` is an hour off in summer). Times are the wall clock of the `datetime` zone, also over DST changes; Polish public holidays (Easter-based ones too) make `workday` and `holiday`. Nothing fires until NTP has set the clock (at most 10 minutes, never before 2025; `:clock({ wait = false })` skips the wait), and moments skipped by a clock jump do not fire later, nor do moments fire twice when the clock steps back; reminders in whole days (`remind = '-1d'`) keep the wall-clock time over a DST change; `catchup = true` starts an event again after a restart or a jump during it. `vclu_draft_test` drives the calendar with its virtual clock. Replaces the `setInterval` that compared `os.date()` in `docs/ai/examples.md` ([#76](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/76)).

### Dashboards

- **A calendar widget** - `:calendar(id, label, cal, { view = 'month' | 'day' })` shows a month grid with the events of each day (holidays and today marked, dots on a phone); a tapped day opens the day: all-day events, then the times with a line at the current time. With `schedules = { 'taryfa' }` the day view draws each schedule as a 24 h bar in the colours the schedule gives its values (`colors = { tania = 'success' }`), the current time marked; `cal:nextChange(id)` and `cal:color(id)` make a status tile such as "tania · do 15:00". Holiday names show in the month and the day (on by default), sunrise and sunset in the day view (on, with a location; to the minute), and the day's name days (off by default); what the widget's options switch off is not sent to the browser, and a viewer can hide the rest on the widget, kept per device, and `cal:day(date)` gives `holidayName` and `namedays` to modules (Polish name days from Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0, see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES). Month and weekday names follow the browser's language ([#76](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/76)).

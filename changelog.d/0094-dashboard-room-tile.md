### Dashboards

- **Room tiles** - `section:room('sypialnia', 'Sypialnia')` is one tile per room: its name, how many lights, dimmers and shown switches are on (a dimmer above 0) and covers open (above 0 or moving), its temperature and humidity with a decimal comma ("2 włączone · 1 otwarta · 21,5 °C · 60 %", or "wszystko wyłączone"). Temperature and humidity default to the area's devices of that kind (the average of several, left out with none); `temperature` / `humidity` take a path or a function for other sensors. A tap opens the room's widgets over the page, picked as `section:area` picks them (`kinds`, `skip`, ids `area:` + path, `#2` for a device shown twice), working through the usual values and actions; the ‹ button, Escape, a tap outside or the system back button closes it (the room adds one history entry, taken off again when it closes), focus stays inside and returns to the tile, a phone shows it full screen. `link` opens a dashboard id, a `/path` or an http(s) URL instead (`//host`, `javascript:` and the like are refused); `disabled` also disables the room's widgets; `title`, `icon`, `span`, `size`, `color` as on other tiles. `dash:floor('Piętro', { rooms = true })` makes one section of room tiles for the floor, one per area in the floor's order (ids `area:@` + the area). Rooms reuse the cache of #92: polls do not refill them, auth, PIN and the app manifest fill nothing; `vclu_dashboards` lists the tile, not the room's widgets. Wrong arguments stop the module with an error naming `section:room` or `dash:floor` at the module's line ([#94](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/94)).

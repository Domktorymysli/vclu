### Grenton

- **A thermostat follows its CLU's firmware** - vCLU asks each CLU with thermostats on the Sync list for its firmware (`get(17)`) and picks the thermostat's features from a table built from Object Manager's device definitions. A thermostat is subscribed only where that is known to be safe: the CLU reports 5.x and its om.lua does not say CLU v1.0 (FwType 4). Everywhere else (firmware 4.x such as 04.08.05, no answer yet) its values are read with `get` every 30 s, one feature at a time and never in the middle of the subscription renewals, until a hardware test shows which features can be subscribed on 4.x. A CLU that answers later with 5.x gets its thermostats subscribed without re-registering anything else. An offline CLU costs one waiting question per start, not one per renewal. Taking a thermostat off the Sync list, or switching Sync off, stops the reads. The log names each CLU, its firmware and the choice once. `clu:firmware()` returns what the CLU reported ([#67](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/67)).

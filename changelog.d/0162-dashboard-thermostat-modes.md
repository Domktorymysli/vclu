### Web

- **The thermostat tile switches modes** - `section:thermostat` shows a row of mode buttons under the target. A `VirtualThermostat` brings manual, holiday (its holiday target, no `+`/`-`) and off (`stop()`; another mode starts it again); `opts.modes` relabels or narrows them and `modes = false` hides them. A table of functions gets a switch with `fns.getMode`, `fns.setMode` and `opts.modes` (a list `{value, label, icon?, readonly?}` or a map `{value = label}`), so a module can offer its own modes, such as a schedule kept by the calendar (`docs/ai/examples.md`). A `RemoteThermostat` still only shows its mode. `readonly` locks the switch too; the `mode` action is refused when the widget is disabled, read-only or the value is not listed ([#162](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/162)).

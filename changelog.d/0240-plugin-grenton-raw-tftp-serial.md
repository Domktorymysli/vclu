### Plugins

- **Direct CLU communication, TFTP and a serial port for plugins** - three new permissions in `plugin.json`, each with `"why"`. `clu` gives `plugin:grentonSocket()` (a UDP socket of the plugin's own: `send`, `broadcast` to the CLU port, `onReceive`) and `plugin:grentonCipher(key, iv)` (the CLU cipher, key and IV raw or in base64). `tftp` gives `plugin:tftp(ip)` with `get` and `put` answering in a callback. `serial` gives `plugin:serialOpen(path, {baud, data, parity, stop})` with `write` and `onData`, on a port the user assigned to the plugin. Installing or updating never allows them: the plugins page asks, says what each reaches, and lets the user limit `clu` and `tftp` to some hosts (with `clu` limited, broadcasts only when the user also allows finding CLUs) and assign the serial ports. A plugin's socket and TFTP client never reach this vclu, its socket cannot bind vclu's ports, and its broadcasts do not reach the vclu it runs in, also right after it closes the socket. Socket, TFTP client and broadcasts are IPv4 only; closing a TFTP handle stops the transfer. Disabling the plugin, or taking the permission away, closes its sockets and ports ([#240](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/240)).

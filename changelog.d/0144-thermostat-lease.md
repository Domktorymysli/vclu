### Lua runtime

- **`VirtualThermostat` can leave the last word to the CLU (`lease`)** - with `lease = 90` a Grenton output (a `RemoteSwitch` DOUT or a `RemoteLight` dimmer) is switched on with `switchOn(90000)` and renewed every quarter of the lease while heating, so when vCLU dies without a stop (`kill -9`, a crash, the server losing power) the CLU switches the relay off by itself within 90 s. Two renewals lost in a row still keep it on; a lost on, or a CLU that restarted, is put right by the next renewal. A renewal is no change: no history entry, no event, and no expected change that would mark a later change from Grenton as vCLU's. Every switch-off stays a plain `switchOff()` and ends the renewals. `lease` below 30 s, or on any other output (virtual, Zigbee, plugins, Grenton RGB lights and LED channels), is an error at `new()`. Off by default: without it the commands are as before ([#144](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/144)).
- **The AI docs say what `VirtualThermostat` guards against and what not** - a "What happens when" table in `docs/ai/examples.md` (the source stops or reports nonsense, a lost command, a reload, vCLU killed, a CLU restart; with and without `lease`), that it is comfort control and the heating's own safety stays, and when to set `maxOn` ([#147](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/147)).

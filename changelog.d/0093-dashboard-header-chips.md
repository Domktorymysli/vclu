### Dashboards

- **Header chips** - `header:chip(id, icon, fn, opts)` puts a small pill in the header bar: the icon in a circle and a short text from `fn` (an alarm time, a running washing machine, who is home, the alarm state). `fn` returns the text, `nil` to hide the chip (it is left out of the values), or `{ text, icon, color }` to change the icon or colour with the state; `opts.color` works as on a status, `opts.link` (an http(s) URL, a `/path` or a dashboard id, which opens `/d/<id>`) makes the chip a keyboard-focusable link, `opts.disabled` as on other widgets. An error in `fn` shows the error mark on the chip, the dashboard keeps working; wrong arguments fail at the module's line with `header:chip(...)` in the message, a chip outside the header is an error; an icon or colour from `fn` that is not a plain name, `#hex` or `rgb()`/`hsl()` is dropped, and a link to `//host` is refused. On a phone the header scrolls sideways, a long text is cut with an ellipsis. Contract: `pkg/lua/dashboard_chips_test.go` ([#93](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/93)).

### HomeKit

- **A Grenton RGB light has its colour in Apple Home** - an exposed `RemoteRGBLight` (LED RGB, LED RGBW) is a colour lightbulb: Hue and Saturation next to On and Brightness, so its colour can be read and set in the Home app. A colour reported by the CLU shows up even when the brightness does not change; Hue and Saturation from the Home app go to the light as one `setColor`. The brightness stays the light's own, and the white of an RGBW module stays as it is (it has no HomeKit characteristic). A colour picked for a light that is off does not switch it on; it is sent when the light is next switched on from the Home app. Dimmers, on/off lights and LED channels are unchanged. Adding the two characteristics changes the bridge's configuration number once, so the Home app reloads the accessories; accessory ids, rooms and automations stay. The exposed list gains `hasColor` and `color` ([#60](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/60)).

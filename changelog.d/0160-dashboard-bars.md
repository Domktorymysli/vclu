### Web

- **Bars widget** - `section:bars(id, label, metricPath, { range = '7d', bucket = '1d', agg = 'delta', unit = 'kWh', compare = 'energia:pv' })` draws one bar per hour or day of the house's zone from `metric()` samples, aggregated by vCLU: `avg` (mean of the samples), `sum`, `max`, `min`, or `delta` for a meter that counts up (a single bad reading adds nothing, a reset or a replaced meter is followed). `compare` puts a second metric beside each bar. A day or hour without data is a gap, today's bar is marked as still filling; a value shows on hover or tap. A public dashboard serves only its widgets' bars, never the raw metric ([#160](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/160)).
- **How long metrics are kept is a setting** - `"metrics": {"retentionDays": 31}` in `.vclu.json` (1-366, default 7 as before); a 30-day bars widget needs 31. A metric sampled every 10 s takes about 200 KB of disk a day, every 60 s about 25 KB, and backups include `metrics/`. `/api/metrics/data`, the dashboard charts and `vclu_metrics` reach back at most that far and answer with at most 2000 points (averaged, same shape). Samples are written with 10 significant digits, so a kWh meter keeps its decimals ([#160](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/160)).

### Dashboards

- **Media player tile** - `section:player(id, label, src, opts)` shows the cover art, title and artist, the state ("odtwarza", "pauza", "wyłączony", "offline", in English too), previous / play-pause / next, mute, power and a volume slider that sends when let go. `src` is a MusicCast device from `Plugin.getPlugin("@vclu/musiccast"):create{...}` (recognised by its methods), or a table of functions for any other player: `getState` returning `{ playing, title, artist, album, artwork, volume, muted, power, online }` and optional `play`, `pause`, `next`, `previous`, `setVolume(percent)`, `setMute(bool)`, `on`, `off`; a missing function hides its button and its action is an error. The volume is checked to be 0-100; only an absolute `http(s)://` artwork URL reaches the page, where it is an `<img>` (never HTML); a picture that does not load shows a note icon and is not asked for again on every poll. The browser loads the artwork from the receiver itself, so it shows only inside the house (no proxy). The MusicCast artwork needs musiccast 0.2.0 ([vclu-plugins#15](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu-plugins/pull/15)). The tile is two columns wide on a desktop and full width on a phone; `opts`: `icon`, `disabled`, `span`, `size`, `color`; an error in `getState` shows the error mark on the tile; wrong arguments fail at the module's line with `section:player(...)` in the message. Contract: `pkg/lua/dashboard_player_test.go` ([#95](https://github.com/Domktorymysli/vclu/issues/95)).

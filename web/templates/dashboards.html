Quick Start

Create modules/dashboards/home.lua with:

Dashboard.new('home')
  :name('Home')
  :icon('house')
  :columns(3)
  :auth('public')
  :section('Living Room', 'lamp')
    :button('lights', 'All Lights', 'lightbulb', function()
      -- Toggle your lights here
    end)
    :toggle('fan', 'Fan', 'fan',
      function() return false end,
      function(v) end)
  :build()

Then visit /d/home to see your dashboard.
